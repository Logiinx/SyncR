SyncR – Privacy policy

SyncR is a personal note app. It has no user accounts and no servers that store your data.

Your notes

Notes, cards, settings and games are stored on your device. If you turn on sync, they are encrypted on your device with your passphrase and stored in your own Dropbox account. Nobody else can read them.

Google Calendar

If you connect Google Calendar, SyncR reads, creates, changes and deletes events in your primary calendar, only to show them in the Reminders tab and to notify you. Events are kept on your device for offline viewing. They are not sent anywhere else, not shared, not sold and not used for ads.

To sign in, a small proxy (a Cloudflare Worker run for SyncR) exchanges the sign-in code from Google for an access key. It passes the key straight back to your device and stores nothing. Your sign-in is kept on your device only. You can remove SyncR's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

SyncR's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
